# Host oracles for SIMPLE's reconstruction as SIMPLE computes it (run `mlxf_oracles3`, on dell2)

Status, 2026-11-24: planned. The maintainer told the batch to proceed, so decisions 1 to 10 stand as
recommended and any of them may be overturned at the review. Base: the maintainer's commit of this note and of
run `mlxf_oracles`' result (the run records its HEAD). SIMPLE's mathematics is stated in section 9 as read at
`d354efee2`. Supersedes: none; it completes [`mlxf_oracles.md`](mlxf_oracles.md). Depends on: dell2 for plain
Fortran (validated: yes, two runs, 2026-11-24); the three oracle modules of `mlxf_oracles` (validated: built
and self-checked on dell2 and on the Mac); section 9's formulas (validated: read from SIMPLE's source by one
reader with line references; items marked as inferences are named in 9.7; not yet run against SIMPLE).

## 1. Why

The first oracles follow the examples note. A review against SIMPLE's source found them free of internal
error, and found six places where SIMPLE computes something else (their plan's last Progress entry). Two
deliverables are affected: K6, the insertion, and K8, the PCG solve, which the maintainer wants on the GPU
and possibly as the default. A kernel that is to replace SIMPLE's code must be judged against SIMPLE's
mathematics. This run adds SIMPLE's forms beside the note's and repairs two checks that could not fail.

## 2. Contract

- **Plain Fortran, no MLX.** Modules under `src/test/` importing intrinsic modules and the existing oracle
  modules' procedures only.
- **The mathematics is section 9's, computed directly in double precision.** SIMPLE applies its vectors in
  single precision; the oracle does not, and says so.
- **The note's forms stay.** Nothing exported by `mlxf_oracle_projection` or `mlxf_oracle_pcg` is removed or
  changed in meaning: the adjoint pair and the note's PCG problem remain valid references for what they
  state. SIMPLE's forms are added under their own names.
- **Held to explicit constructions here.** Every operator is checked against a dense matrix built from
  scalar definitions on the small fixture; every solve against a direct solution.
- **The shared test files are not touched,** and no tester or registry row is added. Constants stay local.
- **The house rules for Fortran hold** (skill `mlxf-modern-fortran`).

### What the check found (2026-11-24)

- **The first oracles export** (their report lists the names): the fixture's geometry, `kb_window`, the
  stencil, the explicit matrix G, the gather, the scatter, the numerator, the density, the packed half with
  its multiplicities, and for the PCG problem the class `oracle_pcg_problem` with its operator,
  preconditioner, Cholesky and reference solve.
- **The oracle's scatter at padded coordinates is SIMPLE's PCG lattice accumulator,** up to SIMPLE's factor 8
  and on every voxel SIMPLE reads (an inference of the reader, to be shown by a check here). SIMPLE's packed
  result is the plain packing of that accumulator, not the oracle's folded one.
- **No check pins the window's `beta`,** and "pack expand" builds its Hermitian field with the mirror the
  expansion uses.
- **Rule `defs`** refuses a constant imported from another oracle; procedures may be imported.

## 9. SIMPLE's reconstruction, as read at `d354efee2`

Stated for the oracle, not as SIMPLE codes it. File names are SIMPLE's, for the maintainer's check.

**Notation.** `N` is the native box (even), `M = 2 N`, `nyq = N / 2`. On every lattice the window is the
first oracles' Kaiser-Bessel window `K` with its `beta`: per axis `b = nint(loc)`, taps `b + j` for
`j = -1, 0, 1`, weights `w(j) = K(loc - b - j) / sum_j K`, and `W` the product of the three axes' weights.
`e_n(d) = w0 + 2 w1 cos(2 pi d / n)` with `w0 = K(0) / (K(0) + 2 K(1))` and `w1 = K(1) / (K(0) + 2 K(1))`.
`F` is the unnormalised forward transform with a negative exponent, `F⁻¹` its normalised inverse.

### 9.1 The gridding backend's insertion (`volume/simple_reconstructor.f90`)

- **Grid.** Native `N³`. The stored half is `h = 0 .. N/2` and `k, m = -N/2 .. N/2 - 1`. The accumulators
  are larger, `h = -3 .. N/2 + 2` and `k, m = -(N/2 + 2) .. N/2 + 2`, addressed by their logical indices
  without any wrap.
- **Plane data** come to the oracle as two arrays per plane: the complex numerator sample `c(h, k)` (the
  plane's transform sampled at even indices of its padded transform, with the shift's phase and the CTF
  factor `t` applied, and `1 / sigma2(shell)` when whitening) and the real `q(h, k) = t²` (with the same
  whitening). They are zero where `nint(sqrt((2h)² + (2k)²)) > N`. They are given for `k ≤ 0`.
- **Pixels inserted:** `h = -N/2 .. N/2`, `k = -N/2 .. N/2 - 1`, with `h² + k² ≤ nyq (nyq + 1)`. For `k > 0`
  the sample is the conjugate mate: `c = conj(c(-h, -k))`, `q = q(-h, -k)`. A pixel is skipped when
  `|Re c| + |Im c| ≤ 1e-10` and `q ≤ 1e-10`, and when all three of its `h` taps are negative.
- **Coordinate:** `loc_j = h R(1, j) + k R(2, j)`, with no padding factor.
- **Deposits:** numerator `+= W · 4 · omega · c`; density `+= W · omega · q`; `omega` is the plane's weight.
  The stencil's weights enter once.
- **Compression** to the stored half: only the stored range is read; the halo and `h < 0` are discarded. For
  `h > 0` the values are taken as they are. On the plane `h = 0`: `S(0, -k, -m) = conj(acc(0, k, m))` and
  `rho(0, -k, -m) = dens(0, k, m)`. A voxel with `|acc| < 1e-10` and `dens ≤ 1e-10` stays zero.
- **Density correction:** `V = 0` where `nint(sqrt(h² + k² + m²)) > nyq`; elsewhere `V = S / rho` if
  `|rho| > 1e-6`, else 0.
- **The map:** the unnormalised inverse transform of `V`, then multiplied per axis by `1 / e_N(x)`, `x` the
  offset from voxel `N/2 + 1` (0 where `|e_N| < 1e-8`).

### 9.2 The PCG backend's lattice insertion (`volume/simple_pcg_lattice.f90`)

- **Grid.** Padded `M³`, the full range, periodic with period `M` on all three axes over
  `[-M/2, M/2 - 1]`. Index `+M/2` lands on `-M/2`.
- **Coordinate:** `loc = 2 [h, k, 0] R`.
- **Pixels:** all `h, k` in `[-N/2, N/2]` with `h² + k² ≤ (N/2)²`; each Friedel mate is its own pixel.
- **Deposits:** numerator `+= W · 8 · t · exp(-i phi) · omega · y(h, k) / sigma2`, with
  `phi = 2 pi (h s1 + k s2) / N` and `y` the plane's transform; density `+= W · t² · omega / sigma2`,
  without the 8. A window lying wholly in `-M/2 < h < 0` is skipped.
- **Packing:** `P(h, k, m) = acc(wrap(h), k, m)` for `h = 0 .. M/2`. There is no conjugate fold.
- **Reading the packed half:** a sample at `h = ±M/2` is read as `conj(P(M/2, -k, -m))`.
- **Against 9.1:** the padded lattice and the factor 2 in the coordinate; a wrap in place of a discarded
  halo; 8 in place of 4; the disk `h² + k² ≤ nyq²` in place of `≤ nyq (nyq + 1)`.

### 9.3 The operator, the ridge and the right-hand side (`volume/simple_reconstructor_pcg.f90`)

`C` embeds the native cube at the centre of the padded one (offset `N/2`); `Cᵀ` crops.

- **The operator:** `H u = P ( Cᵀ F⁻¹ diag(Khat) F C (P u) + lambda (P u) )`.
- **The mask `P`** is binary: 1 where the window is positive. The window, with `r` the distance from voxel
  `N/2 + 1`: `maxrad = min(nint(2 (mskrad + 6)), N) / 2`, `width = 2 (maxrad - mskrad)`,
  `r0 = maxrad - width`; 1 for `r ≤ r0`, 0 for `r ≥ maxrad`, and `(cos(pi (r - r0) / width) + 1) / 2`
  between. Without a support `P` is the identity.
- **The kernel spectrum.** `D` is the packed density of 9.2, kept where `nint(|q|) ≤ N + 3`.
  `Khat = 64 Re F[ F⁻¹[D] / (e_M(d1) e_M(d2) e_M(d3)) ]`, `d` the offset from the real-space origin, with 0
  where the product's modulus is at most 1e-8.
- **The ridge:** given, or `lambda_rel · 64 ·` the mean of `D` over the stored voxels with `|q| ≤ N` and
  `ceiling(|q| / 2) ≤ 6`.
- **The inner product:** the plain sum of products, in double precision.
- **The right-hand side:** `b = P E⁻¹ Cᵀ M³ F⁻¹[packed numerator]`, with
  `E(x) = e_M(x1) e_M(x2) e_M(x3)`, `x` the offset from the native centre, and `E⁻¹ = 0` where
  `|E| < 1e-8`.
- **Start and exit:** `u0 = x / window` where the window is at least 0.1, else 0. The result shipped is
  `window · u`, the soft window applied once, after the solve.

### 9.4 The preconditioner (`apply_precond`)

`z = P E Cᵀ F⁻¹ diag(pi) F C (E r)`, with `E` of 9.3 (1 at the centre).

On the stored voxels with `s = nint(|q|) ≤ N`: `d = max(D, 0)`; `mu_s` is the mean of the positive `D` of
shell `s`; `pi = 1 / (d + 0.01 mu_s)` if `d ≥ 0.1 mu_s`, else 0. For `s > N`, `pi = 0`. The ridge does not
enter.

### 9.5 The solve (`opt/simple_pcg_solver.f90`)

**Start.** A zero right-hand side is an error. With a non-zero start `x`, `r = b - H x`; otherwise `r = b`.
A warm start whose relative residual exceeds a limit (1.0 in the reconstruction) is replaced by zero.
`z = M r`, `p = z`, `rho = r · z`; a non-positive `rho` is an error. The start's residual is never tested
before the first iteration.

**Each iteration,** in this order:
1. `q = H p`, `kappa = p · q`. If `kappa` is not finite or not positive: stop "indefinite", with `x` as it
   was.
2. `alpha = rho / kappa`; `x += alpha p`; `r -= alpha q`.
3. If the iteration number is a multiple of 25: `r = b - H x`.
4. The `rtol` test: `|r| / |b| ≤ rtol` (1e-4). The `xtol` test:
   `|alpha| |p| / max(|x|, epsilon) ≤ 1.5e-2`. Both only when `rtol > 0`.
5. Stop if either holds or the iteration number is `maxits` (50). The reason is "rtol" before "xtol";
   otherwise "maxits", or "fixed iterations" when `rtol ≤ 0`.
6. `z = M r`, `rho' = r · z`, `beta = rho' / rho`, `p = z + beta p`, `rho = rho'`.

Returned: `x`, the reason, the iteration count, the first and last relative residuals and their history.
SIMPLE forms the dots in double precision and applies the vectors, `alpha` and `beta` in single; the oracle
is double throughout.

### 9.6 The projector's index map at the grid's edge (`image/simple_projector.f90`)

The projector reads `F(c1(h), c23(k), c23(m))` for `h, k, m` in `[-M/2 - 2, M/2 + 2]`, through the conjugate
mate when `c1(h) < 0`. `c23` is periodic with period `M`. `c1` is not:

| `h` | `-M/2 - 2` | `-M/2 - 1` | `-M/2 .. M/2` | `M/2 + 1` | `M/2 + 2` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `c1(h)` | `M/2 - 1` | `M/2` | `h` | `-M/2` | `-M/2 + 1` |
| the periodic image | `M/2 - 2` | `M/2 - 1` | `h` | `-M/2 + 1` | `-M/2 + 2` |

The scalar and the batch projector use the same map. With `|loc| = 2 k`, no axis reaches the edge for
`kto ≤ N/2 - 1`. On the shell `k = N/2` the taps that differ are reached when `|loc_1| ≥ M/2 - 0.5`.
